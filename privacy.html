Privacy Policy for Family Care (ShareLoc)

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Family Care (ShareLoc) helps families share their location with each other and organize family activities in a shared Google Sheet. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how you can delete it.

1. Data We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we collect:

  • Your name, email address, and profile picture (from Google profile - basic profile info)
  • Location data you choose to share with your family group
  • Family group data you create (member names, shared notes)

2. Google Drive and Google Sheets Data

We use the Google Drive API with the limited drive.file scope. This means the app can only access files it creates or that you explicitly open with the app. We do NOT access all your Drive files.

We create one Google Sheet for your family group to store shared locations and family data. We do not read, modify, or delete any other files in your Drive.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and display your profile in the app
  • To show your shared location only to members of your family group
  • To create and update the shared family Google Sheet
  • To keep the app working and fix bugs

4. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising. Your location is shared only with members of your family group that you choose.

Data is stored in Google Drive (your own account) and on your device. We do not store your data on our own external servers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Delete your family group from inside the app
  • Delete the shared Google Sheet from your Google Drive
  • Remove app access from your Google account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

If you delete your account, all data created by the app in your Drive will remain under your control until you delete it from Drive.

6. Security

We use Google OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication. We never see or store your Google password.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for families and must be used by adults or with parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new updated date.
